# Himmat Tea / Godgifted — Page-by-Page Content Rewrite

Companion to the Content Audit & Reduction Plan. This document goes page by page: what's there now, exactly what to cut, and suggested rewritten copy you can drop in directly. Facts, numbers, and claims are preserved — only the wording is tightened.

One correction from the initial audit: Careers has **6 real job listings**, not 19 (the earlier scan counted values/benefits/jobs together). The listings themselves are legitimate — the fix there is trimming each one, not questioning whether they're real.

---

## 1. Home Page

**Current structure:** Hero (headline + subhead + 2 CTAs) → Product Slider → Product Lines Showcase → Products Section → Features (multiple benefit tiles) → CTA block → Testimonials → Footer.

**Problem:** Hero, Features, and the CTA block each independently make the "why Himmat" pitch, so a visitor reads the same argument three times before reaching testimonials.

**Keep:** Hero headline + one subhead line. One CTA block, placed once — ideally right before Testimonials, not duplicated near the top too.

**Cut:** Any second paragraph under the Hero subhead. Reduce Features to 3 tiles max, one line each. Remove standalone intro copy above Testimonials ("What our customers say" is enough of a header).

**Suggested Features section (3 tiles, down from current set):**

| Icon | Current style | Rewritten |
|---|---|---|
| Leaf | *(multi-line)* | **Direct from the source** — Sourced straight from Himalayan farms, no middlemen. |
| Award/Check | *(multi-line)* | **Lab-tested, organically grown** — Every lot passes cupping and third-party testing. |
| Truck | *(multi-line)* | **Ships worldwide** — Nepal-wide in 3–5 days, international in 10–14. |

**Suggested CTA block copy:**
> **Taste the Himalayas.** Free shipping in Nepal on orders over Rs. 3,000. [Shop Teas] [Explore Godgifted Dal]

That's it — no restated brand story. The brand story belongs on About/Sourcing, which the Hero and nav should link to for anyone who wants it.

## 3. Sourcing Page

**Current:** ~550 words. Strong, specific content — this is some of the best copy on the site — but re-opens with the same "From the Hills of Nepal" framing as About, and each of the 4 process steps runs long.

**Cut the intro to one line** (don't re-tell the founding story — link to About instead):
> **How We Source**
> Four checkpoints stand between a Himalayan farm and your cup. Here's what each one does.

**Rewritten process steps (currently 2–3 sentences each, tightened to one):**

1. **How we pick partner farms** — We visit every farm ourselves and only work with growers we'd invite you to see in person.
2. **First-flush black and green** — We harvest at peak flush, using traditional hand-plucking for the finest grades.
3. **Cupping and lab tests** — Every lot passes a sensory cupping and third-party lab test for pesticides, heavy metals, and microbial safety before it ships.
4. **Eco-friendly, nitrogen-flushed** — Nitrogen-flushed, food-safe pouches; FSC-certified outer packaging; consolidated, carbon-offset freight.

**Keep as-is (already tight and scannable):** the estate spotlight cards (Guranse Estate, Kanchanjangha Tea Estate, etc.) and the certification badges list — these are specific and differentiated; don't cut facts here, only surrounding filler if any exists.

## 4. FAQ Page

**Current:** ~820 words across 3 categories (Orders & Shipping, Products & Brewing, Subscriptions). Good structure, but several answers pack 2–3 sub-answers into one block.

**Keep the 3-category structure and every question — this is genuinely useful, search-friendly content.** Tighten answers only:

| Question | Current (multi-clause) | Rewritten |
|---|---|---|
| Domestic shipping time? | "Orders within Nepal are delivered in 3–5 business days. We ship from Kathmandu and cover all major cities and towns. Remote areas may take an additional 1–2 days." | "3–5 business days across Nepal, shipped from Kathmandu. Remote areas may add 1–2 days." |
| International shipping? | "Yes! We ship worldwide. International delivery typically takes 10–14 business days depending on your location and local customs processing. You will receive a tracking number once your order is dispatched." | "Yes — worldwide, in 10–14 business days depending on customs. You'll get a tracking number at dispatch." |
| Return policy? | *(4 sentences covering window, condition, contact, exception)* | "Returns accepted within 7 days, unopened and in original packaging. Email hello@himmattea.com with your order number. Opened tea can't be returned for hygiene reasons." |
| Shelf life? | *(3 sentences: shelf life, post-opening, storage advice)* | "2 years sealed; once opened, best within 6 months. Store airtight, away from light and heat." |

Apply the same tightening pattern across the remaining ~14 questions: one fact-dense sentence, occasionally two if the answer genuinely has two parts (e.g. brewing temperatures by tea type, which is a table candidate — see below).

**Consider converting the brewing-temperature answer into a small table** instead of a paragraph — it's inherently tabular data (Green 70–80°C, White 75–85°C, Oolong 85–95°C, Black/Pu-erh 95–100°C, Herbal 100°C) and will scan far faster than prose.

**Current:** ~1,160 and ~850 words. This is a reformatting job more than a cutting job — legal substance shouldn't be thinned out, but dense paragraphs should become scannable sections.

**Fix:**
- Add a short table of contents at the top linking to each section (Nepal Ecommerce Act / GDPR-relevant sections likely already exist — keep the substance).

## 10. Navigation (Mega-Menu)

**Current:** ~970 words of copy embedded in the navigation component — full product names, region subtext ("Zhejiang, China," "West Bengal, India"), and three rotating promo banner messages, all inside the dropdown itself.

**Fix:** A mega-menu should orient, not narrate.

- **Product entries:** name only, no region subtext in the menu (region/origin belongs on the product/collection page). Cuts each entry from ~2 lines to 1.
- **Promo banner:** keep one message at a time, not three competing ones:
  - Current: "Free shipping on orders over Rs. 3,000," "Spring Harvest 2026 is here," "Wholesale pricing for cafés, hotels & retailers" — all live simultaneously.
  - Rewritten: rotate through these on a timer if you want variety, but show one at a time — don't stack three asks on a first-time visitor before they've even reached a page.
- **Category labels:** keep short and functional (Teas / Godgifted Dal / Brewing Guides / Wholesale) — no descriptive taglines needed in a dropdown.

This single change affects every page on the site, since the nav is shared — highest-leverage item on the whole list for effort spent.

## Quick-reference: what NOT to cut anywhere

- Specific numbers (shipping days, MOQs, discounts, certifications, prices) — these are the site's credibility signals and its SEO differentiation. Cut the sentence *around* a number, never the number.
- Named estates, farms, and people (Guranse Estate, Kanchanjangha, Raj/Priya/Aarav) — specificity beats generic brand language every time.
- FAQ question phrasing — it matches real search queries; only the answers should shrink.
- Legal substance in Terms/Privacy — reformat, don't remove.
